## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is a small cultural seal or totem, not a traditional ornament and not a souvenir illustration. Compress the subject to one memorable contour and structural relation. A circle, square, fine engraved line or light woodblock texture may frame it, without stacking pattern. Keep the mark small and grave inside a large field. Material reads as antique paper, seal ink and print grain. Color is a short Eastern palette drawn from the photograph: cinnabar, antique gold, warm ivory, ink black or celadon, never festive clutter. Type is a short colophon at a distance from the mark. Reject a complete scene, mascot, piled traditional motifs, cartoon rendering and a template logo.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-270/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
